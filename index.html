About me

I am a student of Digital Business and Entrepreneurship at IIM Bangalore. I did my 11th and 12th in the Science stream with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics, which taught me to take a problem apart before trying to solve it.

Outside the classroom I have always been on stage or behind it. I have hosted school events, performed in dance, built a science exhibition project and served as Discipline Captain in Class XII.

These experiences taught me to speak confidently, work with a team and stay calm when an event is live, and I bring that into my studies in digital business, design and video.

Discipline Captain

In Class XII I was the school's Discipline Captain for a full year. The role was about setting the standard for other students and helping the school run smoothly every day.

  • Kept assemblies, corridors and events orderly
  • Supported teachers during school programmes and exams
  • Acted as a link between students and staff
  • Led by example on punctuality and conduct
  • Dealt with situations calmly and fairly, and reported serious matters to teachers

What it taught me

Responsibility, fair decision making, handling people and staying composed under pressure. These help in group work and in leading a team.
