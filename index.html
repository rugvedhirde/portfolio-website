About Me

I study Digital Business and Entrepreneurship at IIM Bangalore. Before this, I did my 11th and 12th in the Science stream with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics, which is where I picked up a habit of breaking problems into smaller parts.

What interests me most is how a business reaches people online. That covers how an app or website is laid out, how a video is cut so people keep watching, and how all of it supports an idea someone is trying to build.

My degree lets me look at these things from both sides, the business side and the design side, and I try to use both in the work I do.
