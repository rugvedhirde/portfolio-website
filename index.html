About me

I am a student of Digital Business and Entrepreneurship at IIM Bangalore. I did my 11th and 12th in the Science stream with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics, which taught me to take a problem apart before trying to solve it.

My interest is in the full journey of an idea: the business model behind it, the design that makes it easy to use, and the visuals and video that present it.

My programme lets me study the business side and the digital side together, and I try to use both in everything I make.
